The Process

How It's Made

From farm to bottle, discover the journey of Amul Cool Rose.

1. Pure Sourcing

We start with 100% pure, farm-fresh milk collected daily from our trusted dairy cooperatives.

2. Floral Infusion

Natural, fragrant rose extracts are carefully blended with the milk to create our signature refreshing flavor.

3. Flash Chilling

The infused milk is instantly flash-chilled to lock in its fresh taste and essential nutrients without preservatives.

4. Premium Bottling

Finally, it is sealed in sterilized, eco-friendly glass bottles, ready to cool you down.
